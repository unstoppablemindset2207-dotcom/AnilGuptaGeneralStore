About Us

Anil Gupta General Store is a trusted and well-established retail shop located in Lumla, District Tawang, Arunachal Pradesh. With more than 20 years of experience in serving customers, the store has built a strong reputation for reliability, affordability, and consistent quality.


We aim to provide all essential daily-use products under one roof so that customers do not need to visit multiple shops. From grocery and food items to personal care and household products, everything is available conveniently at one place.


Our store is known for offering products at reasonable prices without compromising on quality. Over the years, we have maintained strong relationships with our customers by providing friendly service and dependable availability of goods.


We continuously strive to improve our services and ensure that every customer leaves satisfied. Our long-standing presence in the area reflects the trust and loyalty of the community we serve.
